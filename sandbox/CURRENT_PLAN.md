# Active Sandbox Plan: Lineage Floor & Cumulative Build Rank (Option 1 + Option 2)

Tested and integrated a dual-layer system combining:
1. **Option 2 (Lineage Floor & Wheel Pity)**: High-tier traits (God-Tier, Mythic, Legendary) guarantee a minimum rank floor (Rank A, B, or C) by upgrading lower slices on the 7th wheel before spinning.
2. **Option 1 (Cumulative Build Score)**: The fighter's overall letter rank badge (Rank E to EX) is derived from their total build power score (Race + Weapon + Powers + Stats + Rank Mult), ensuring godly builds are never demoted by a low spin.
3. **Fair Baseline**: Rank E's multiplier raised from 0.5x to 0.9x with Underdog potential.
