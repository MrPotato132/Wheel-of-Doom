## Core Mechanics Overview

1. **Character Generation**: Characters roll across several core wheels to determine their fundamental identity:
   - **Race**: Species and physiological base.
   - **Archetype**: Combat class, modifier perks, or mechanical playstyle (originally introduced in Season 3 to balance characters whose race falls below Elementals).
   - **Stats**: Strength, Speed, Durability, IQ, Fighting Capabilities, Power Mastery, and Chaos Force.
   - **Powers, Weapons, Enchants & Weaknesses**: Rolled on dedicated accessory wheels.
2. **Sub-Wheels**: Many races and archetypes trigger specialized secondary wheels that determine lineage, ranks, companion beasts, unique gear, or magical disciplines.

## Archetypes & Modifier Wheels

### Slayer
*One of the most competitive archetypes for lower-tier races.*
- Spins a target "Prey" Race.
- **Perk**: Gains **+2 to all stats** whenever fighting against their chosen prey race.

---

### Retired / Legacy Archetypes
- **Mutant (Old)**: Maxed out a single stat (Force Phenom [Strength], Speedster [Speed], Regenerative Tank [Durability], Super Genius [IQ], Berserker [Fighting], Power Nexus [Power Mastery], Chaos Catalyst [Chaos Force]).
- **Dual Wielder**: Spun the Weapon wheel twice and received two enchantments with one mastery roll.
- **Chaos Agent**: Granted guaranteed Chaos Force (later reworked/merged into Corrupted).
- **Ethan's Bloodcult**: Dedicated cult faction formed after Ethan's dominant S4 tournament run.
